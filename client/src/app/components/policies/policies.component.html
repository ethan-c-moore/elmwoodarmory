Policies

Returns

We do not accept returns or refunds on custom, made-to-order, inventory, or personalized items. If there is an extraneous circumstance, please email us at business@elmwoodarmory.com.

Elmwood Armory is not responsible for products that do not fit due to incorrect measurements provided by the customer. Please double-check your measurements before submitting them for custom pieces.

Custom Pieces

Once a final concept for a custom piece is approved, deposit has been made and the project started, any changes that require replacing leather will incur an additional fee. Taking a deposit allows us to purchase the leather and hardware needed to begin your project. Once the materials have been purchased and the work started, this deposit is non-refundable.

Damages and Repairs

Inspect your item upon arrival and contact me within 7 days of delivery is there are any defects, damage, or if you received the wrong item. We’ll evaluate the issue and do our best to make it right.

Damage caused by water, pets, misuse, lack of maintenance, or general wear and tear is not covered. However, we’re happy to help with repairs any way we can for a small fee. To maintain your leather, we recommend cleaning them with leather soap and conditioning them annually.

Cancellations

If your item hasn’t shipped yet, we may be able to cancel the order. Once the item has shipped, cancellations are no longer possible. If you have a special circumstance, please reach out.

Shipping

Ready-Made Items

Ready-Made Items are in-stock inventory and typically ship within 5 business days of your order. If we anticipate any delay, we’ll notify you by email. Made-to-Order and Custom Items

Items that are made-to-order or custom are crafted to your specifications. Because of the time and care involved, we cannot guarantee exact delivery dates.

  • Smaller made-to-order items usually ship within a month.
  • Custom orders will be completed in the order they are received.

We’ll always stay in touch with updates and let you know when we begin and finish your piece.

Multiple orders with the same customer name and shipping address will be combined into one shipment. If you’d prefer your order to ship separately, email Lalenia.moore@elmwoodarmory.com with your request.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how we collect and use your personal information when you make a purchase from www.elmwoodarmory.com (the “Site”).

Personal Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Payment Information (including credit card numbers)
  • Email address and phone number

If you submit a custom order or request a service, we also collect your name and preferred contact information (email or phone number).

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Communicate with you about or order or custom project
  • Send invoices or order confirmations
  • Handle any order issues or questions

We will never sell or share your personal information with third parties.
